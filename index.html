Getting There

Travel & Stay

Vilnius

  • By air: Vilnius International Airport (VNO) is about 15 minutes from the Old Town by taxi or ride-hailing app.
  • Where to stay: the Old Town (UNESCO World Heritage) is walkable, charming and close to everything.
  • Good to know: Lithuania uses the euro and is in the Schengen area.

Marsanne

  • By air: Lyon–Saint Exupéry (LYS, ~1h45 drive) or Marseille Provence (MRS, ~2h drive).
  • By train: TGV to Valence TGV (~45 min) or regional trains to Montélimar (~20 min), then car or taxi.
  • Getting around: we recommend renting a car — the village sits among hills and vineyards.

Between the two

  • Direct and one-stop flights connect Vilnius with Lyon, Marseille and Paris.
  • From Paris, the TGV reaches Valence in about 2h15.
  • August is peak season in France — we suggest booking early.

Do I need to attend both events?

Not at all. We would love to see you wherever you can make it — Vilnius, Marsanne, or both. Please let us know in your RSVP.

What should I wear?

For Vilnius, elegant daywear. For the reception in Marsanne, summer formal — think light fabrics and comfortable shoes for stone paths and dancing on the terrace. August in the Drôme is warm, often above 30°C.

Where should I stay in Marsanne?

We will share a list of recommended accommodation nearby soon. Marsanne is small, so we recommend booking early.

Can I bring a plus-one or children?

Your invitation will specify who is included. If you have any questions, just reach out to us.
